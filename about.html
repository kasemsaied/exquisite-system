What we do

ESI delivers aluminum and shading scopes for residential, commercial and industrial projects. Our core activities include:

  • Aluminum windows, doors, shopfronts and façades.
  • Curtains, roller blinds and interior shading solutions.
  • Manual and motorized shutter systems (security & light control).
  • Turn-key supply & installation, aligned with project schedule.
