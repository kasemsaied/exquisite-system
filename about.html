About Exquisite System Industry Company (ESI)

Exquisite System Industry Company (ESI) is a Saudi industrial company specialized in aluminum systems, shading solutions and shutter systems. The company supports residential, commercial and industrial projects across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia through practical engineering solutions, technical documentation and coordinated execution.

ESI works closely with engineering consultants, main contractors and project owners, providing integrated scopes aligned with project specifications, quality standards and delivery schedules.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be a trusted technical partner for aluminum, shading and shutter systems across Saudi Arabia, recognized for clarity, coordination and execution quality.

Our Mission

To deliver compliant, practical and cost-effective solutions that meet project requirements, technical specifications and timelines.
